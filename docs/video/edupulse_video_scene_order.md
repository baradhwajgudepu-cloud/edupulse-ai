## Audio Architecture & Sound Design

### 1. Background Music (BGM) Track
- **Recommended Genre**: Modern Corporate Tech / Ambient Electronic (similar to Ólafur Arnalds meets Tycho, or modern SaaS launch tracks).
- **Tempo**: 115 BPM steady pulse in 4/4 time.
- **Key Progression**:
  - *Act 1 (0:00 – 0:18)*: D minor pad, spacious, contemplative.
  - *Act 2 (0:18 – 1:03)*: Transitions to D major, introduces warm analog synthesizer pulse and crisp rhythmic hi-hats.
  - *Act 3 (1:03 – 1:48)*: Driving electronic bassline and melodic marimba accents to reflect operational momentum.
  - *Act 4 (1:48 – 2:24)*: Inspiring string crescendo joining the synthesizer rhythm, concluding on a sustained resonant root chord.

### 2. Voiceover Mixing
- Centered mono track, EQ smoothed with a gentle presence boost at 3.5kHz.
- Sidechain ducking configured with 20ms attack and 250ms release, lowering the BGM track by -16dB whenever voiceover is active.

### 3. Foley & SFX Palette
- UI clicks: Crisp 48kHz wooden/glass micro-clicks (subtle, non-intrusive).
- Transitions: Soft whooshes and low-frequency sweeps (cut below 60Hz to prevent muddy bass).
- Milestones: Pleasant two-note major-third chime on validation success and payment confirmation.

## Video Editor Assembly Playbook (DaVinci Resolve / Premiere Pro / Final Cut)

1. **Timeline Setup**:
   - Create a 1920 × 1080 (or 3840 × 2160) sequence at 29.97 or 30.00 FPS.
   - Set color space to Rec.709.

2. **Clip Placement**:
   - Lay down `clip_01` through `clip_16` sequentially on Video Track 1.
   - Trim each clip precisely to 9 seconds (270 frames at 30 FPS).
   - Apply 6 to 12 frame cross-dissolves or whip pans according to the master sequencing table.

3. **Audio Alignment**:
   - Place Voiceover stem on Audio Track 1.
   - Place BGM stem on Audio Track 2.
   - Place SFX stems on Audio Tracks 3 and 4.
   - Align voiceover starts at approximately second 1.0 of each 9-second clip to allow 1 second of visual establishment before speech begins.

4. **Export Settings**:
   - Codec: H.264 / H.265 (MP4 container)
   - Profile: High, Level 5.1
   - Bitrate: 25 Mbps VBR 2-pass (1080p) or 50 Mbps (4K)
   - Audio: AAC 320 kbps, 48 kHz Stereo.
