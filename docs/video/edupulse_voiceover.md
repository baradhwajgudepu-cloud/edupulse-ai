# EduPulse AI — Master Voiceover Production Script

**Project Title**: EduPulse AI Product Demonstration Video  
**Target Duration**: 2 Minutes 24 Seconds (144 Seconds across 16 Clips)  
**Tone & Style**: Confident, Modern, Warm, Professional, Enterprise EdTech  
**Pacing Benchmark**: 130 – 140 Words Per Minute (Natural conversational cadence with deliberate pauses)  
**Strict Editorial Policy**:
- *No exaggerated superlatives* (e.g., avoid "100% infallible", "guaranteed highest grades", "replaces educators").
- *Grounded in real features* (highlight real operational workflows, real data integrations, real location boundaries, and verified role connectivity).

---

## Audio Production & Vocal Direction Guidelines

1. **Voice Persona**:
   - Mature, articulate, authoritative yet approachable.
   - Tone resembles modern enterprise innovators (e.g., Apple Keynote, Stripe, Linear, Google Cloud).
   - Natural, conversational inflection with clear articulation on operational terms.

2. **Microphone & Engineering Specs**:
   - **Recommended Mic**: Large-diaphragm condenser (Neumann U87, Shure SM7B, or Sennheiser MKH 416).
   - **Audio Format**: 24-bit / 48kHz WAV uncompressed.
   - **Processing**: Mild high-pass filter at 80Hz, gentle optical compression (2:1 ratio), subtle de-essing, neutral room acoustics (sub-20dB noise floor).
   - **Music Ducking**: Automatic side-chain ducking of background ambient music by -14dB to -18dB whenever speech is active.

3. **Pronunciation & Terminology Guide**:
   - **EduPulse AI**: `[EH-joo-puls AY-EYE]` (Not "Edu-pulse" with a hard D; smooth and fluid).
   - **Student 360**: `[STOO-dent THREE-SIX-TEE]`
   - **Geofencing**: `[JEE-oh-fen-sing]`
   - **CBSE / ICSE**: Spelled out as single capital letters `[SEE-BEE-ESS-EE]`
   - **Timetable**: `[TYM-tay-bul]`
